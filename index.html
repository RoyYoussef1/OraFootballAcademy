The academy

A football home, not a drop-off

ORA is independently built and run by people who coach for a living. Every session belongs to a written curriculum, every age group has a plan for the season, and every player gets feedback they can act on next week - the same standards at all three venues.

Develop

Technical, tactical, physical and mental work, built up block by block through the season instead of drill by drill.

Inspire

Coaches who know each child's name, standards that feel like a club, and an environment kids want to come back to.

Achieve

Leagues, tournaments and friendlies so progress is measured where it counts in matches, against real opposition.

Programmes

Choose the group that fits your child

Boys and girls, grouped by age so every player trains at the right level. Same methodology at every venue, scaled to the age.

4–7

Grassroots

First touches, first friendships. Fun-led sessions that build coordination and confidence.

  • 45–60 minute sessions
  • Small groups, high ball contact
  • Games every session
8–12

Development

The core years. Technique under pressure, game understanding and the habits of a team player.

  • Position-aware training
  • Termly progress feedback
  • Match play built in
13–18

Performance

For players who want more. Intensity, physical preparation and a competitive match calendar.

  • Strength and conditioning
  • Video and match analysis
  • League and tournament football
Camps

Holiday camps

Winter and summer multi-day camps that keep players sharp through the school break.

  • Full or half-day options
  • Open to non-members
  • Tournament finish day
